Nelson Mandela Profile

Nelson Mandela

Nelson Mandela was a South African leader, activist, and former president. He fought against apartheid and became a symbol of peace, equality, and freedom.

Personal Information

  • Full Name: Nelson Rolihlahla Mandela
  • Born: July 18, 1918
  • Country: South Africa
  • Profession: Politician and Activist

Major Achievements

  1. Fought against apartheid in South Africa
  2. Spent 27 years in prison for justice and equality
  3. Became the first Black president of South Africa
  4. Won the Nobel Peace Prize in 1993
